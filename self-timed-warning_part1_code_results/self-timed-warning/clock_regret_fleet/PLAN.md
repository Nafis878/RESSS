# Complete heterogeneous-fleet selection: locked investigation plan

The requested target is a stronger scientific contribution suitable for RESS,
not an arbitrary score increase. Earlier evidence and PDFs are frozen.

## Hypothesis fixed before implementation outcomes

For earlier-versus-later ages a<b, arbitrary positive rational failure premiums
require choosing a subset of eligible censored records, rather than a horizon
prefix. At fixed cardinality, its sufficient statistics are total premium Q and
total earliest lifetime S. The objective increases with Q and decreases with S.
A cardinality-indexed approximate Pareto dynamic program may preserve S exactly
in the favorable direction while approximating Q. Combine it with the proved
exact later-pair routine to obtain a complete-family FPTAS, explicit witnesses,
score intervals and a multiplicative decision certificate.

Derive all statements before interpreting experiments. Reject a general novelty
claim for Pareto trimming: approximation schemes and one-exact approximate Pareto
sets are established. Audit the actual specialized theorem and its assumptions.
Do not claim NP-hardness without a valid reduction.

## Validation and planned evidence

1. Independently enumerate 1,000 small integer full-clock instances, arbitrary
   pair order and rational premiums, seed 20261031. Compare exact Pareto values,
   feasible approximate witnesses and rigorous lower/upper intervals.
2. Compare complete-family approximate scores and selected-policy guarantees
   against full-clock family enumeration for 200 small cases, seed 20261032.
3. Run 200 common-cost reductions against the frozen exact selector, seed
   20261033. Test permutation invariance, invalid inputs, tied costs, no censoring,
   empty eligibility, both pair orders, and nontrivial trimming.
4. Check continuous earlier-pair closure against independent endpoint-phase
   enumeration for 200 cases, seed 20261034; preserve phase labels and explicitly
   distinguish a supremum from an attained witness.
5. Replay all 304 previously frozen real cohorts. First verify common-cost
   reduction. Then assign the explicit PUBLIC scenario premium
   `(rho-1)*(1 + unit_id mod 3)` to each asset. These premiums are scenario values,
   not measured failure consequences. Use epsilon=1/4. Report all cohorts,
   score intervals, selection ambiguity, certified decisions and realized
   cohort regret from sealed lifetimes only after selecting policies.
   This is a cost-scenario replay using engineering lifetime data, not evidence
   of deployed fleet benefits or a claim of population generalization.
6. Report approximation gaps and state counts at tolerances 1/2, 1/4, 1/10 on
   30 fixed heterogeneous stress cases, seed 20261035. Use exact Pareto values
   where feasible. No runtime significance claim without a separately planned
   timing study; no tests of historical novelty through p-values.

If a planned experiment fails, retain the failure and correction log. Do not
tune premiums, epsilon or seeds after seeing performance to favor the method.
Publish the mathematical assumptions, standard ingredients, unsuccessful
comparisons, limitations and verdict. Completing these phases cannot guarantee
9/10 historical originality or journal acceptance.
