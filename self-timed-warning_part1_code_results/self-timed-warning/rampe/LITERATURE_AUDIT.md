# RAMPE literature and novelty audit — 8 October 2026

## Search protocol and reading scope

Two rounds were performed. Before running the prototypes, searches covered dynamic ensemble competence, META-DES, local experts, cost-sensitive multi-expert deferral, confidence/stability selection, conformal risk control and predictive-maintenance RL. After implementing the actual gate, searches targeted competence-threshold arbitration, confidence in meta-model competence, abstaining dynamic ensembles, safe baseline fallback, policy-as-arm arbitration, renewal reward/time ratios, truncated feedback, distribution shift and maintenance partial observation. The second round found particularly close streaming arbitrators and renewal-policy bandits. The name RAMPE was not used as evidence of novelty.

Search families included: “dynamic ensemble selection competence uncertainty shift”; “META-DES dynamic weighting”; “learning to defer multiple experts confidence calibration”; “policy selection confidence baseline safe exploration”; “Arbitrated Dynamic Ensemble with Abstaining”; “renewal bandit ratio reward cost”; “Learning to Control Renewal Processes with Bandit Feedback”; “reinforcement learning maintenance partial observability”; “meta reinforcement learning maintenance transfer”; “distributionally robust model selection”; “stability generalization”; and “hyperparameter temporal distribution shifts”. Major-venue proceedings, publisher pages, arXiv versions and author manuscripts were used. Third-party snippets located papers but are not the technical evidence below.

This is a targeted prior-art audit, not a systematic-review claim or proof that every paper has been found. A negative method verdict needs established reproducing mechanisms, not proof of literature completeness. Key full formulations read: META-DES; multi-expert deferral; DeCCaF; streaming ADE workshop manuscript; renewal bandits; LTT; Pareto testing; confidence/stability cross-validation. Abstract/overview-only readings are explicitly marked. Mathematical cells below are compact explanatory formulations, not claimed verbatim equations unless a location is stated. “Invalidates” concerns a particular broad novelty claim; it does not assert every paper is identical to this maintenance implementation.

## Strict novelty gate

| Gate | Assessment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| A: meaningfully different scientific problem | FAIL for the implemented claim | Known contextual competence/deferral problem applied to maintenance; operational censored feedback is not implemented. |
| B: meaningfully different mathematics | FAIL | Finite-family risk testing, resampling sensitivity, uncertainty penalization, logistic meta-competence. |
| C: meaningfully different decision mechanism | FAIL | Select competent experts, constrain risk, fall back when evidence is weak. |
| D: existing methods cannot trivially reproduce it | FAIL | A cost-sensitive dynamic-expert selector with a risk filter and baseline fallback reproduces the architecture. Exact coefficient/threshold identity is not necessary. |
| E: difference matters scientifically | FAIL for a new method | Real-data authority never changes from 1-SE; no incremental real effect. Toy contextual gains are familiar and fragile under shift. |
| F: literature does not establish the same mechanism | FAIL at the conceptual level | META-DES, streaming ADE, calibrated multi-expert deferral and safe policy-as-arm arbitration cover the claimed ingredients. No priority claim for the precise six-feature recipe is made. |

The negative audit remains useful: it prevents attribution of fallback gains to the gate, distinguishes source certainty from transfer reliability, and records a renewal-objective routing mismatch. These findings are not established here as a first-in-literature methodological contribution. Changing weights, naming an RL variant, or substituting PPO does not change the verdict.

## Minimum meaningful change

Specify an identifiable operational problem where maintenance authority changes what can be observed: policy-induced right censoring, delayed failures, paid inspections, random replacement durations and a joint renewal-rate/conditional-risk objective. Show which assumptions identify the risks, which existing renewal-bandit, safe-deferral or maintenance-POMDP method fails, and why. A new estimator, impossibility result or regret/safety tradeoff for that precisely defined distinction could support novelty. This is a candidate direction requiring a fresh audit, not a promise that combining these terms is new. The current prototype does not solve it.
