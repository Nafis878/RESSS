# Equivalence and priority audit for the stronger result

Audit date: 8 October 2026. The claim now concerns the **two-world integer theorem, one-world continuous supremum, exact O(n log n+K^2 n) solver, and resolution-factor bound**. The frozen quadratic method and its prior audit are available in `../clock_regret/LITERATURE.md`; this supplement does not alter them.

## Defensible claim and known ingredients

We derive a two-world extremal characterization of later-versus-earlier finite-cohort age-replacement rate ratios from shared right-censored lifetimes, combine it with a sorted-prefix earlier comparison to obtain an exact solver, and derive a continuous counterpart and a uniform1+1/r clock-refinement bound. These exact specialized statements were developed and implemented in this research.

Age replacement, common potential outcomes, minimax relative regret, nonrectangular policy uncertainty, optimization over missing outcomes, fractional programming, concave-function endpoint minima, and discretization arguments are established concepts. The claim is their specific mathematical consequence and computational structure in this stated model, not their invention. We found no matching theorem in the inspected works; that supports a narrow **proposed originality claim**, not conclusive historical priority.
