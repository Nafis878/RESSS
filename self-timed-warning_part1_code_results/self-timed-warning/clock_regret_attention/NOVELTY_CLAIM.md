# Precisely defined claim and decision gate

## Claim proposed for specialist review

For fixed-age replacement on a right-censored integer-clock finite cohort with
common preventive cost and heterogeneous positive rational failure premiums,
we derive an omitted-premium factor for restricted adversarial subset search
of a same-world cost-rate ratio. The factor is valid for any focus selector,
is asymptotically sharp when only the specified cost summaries are retained,
and yields a premium-optimal focus and minimum certified focus budget.
Intersecting this interval with a complete-family FPTAS preserves compatible
attaining witnesses and its (1+epsilon) policy-selection guarantee. Attention
is an optional computational proposal and is evaluated against retrained
pointwise/pooling/context ablations and sequential greedy.

This wording defines the contribution without asserting historical firstness.
The precise A1 formula, family and assumptions are in THEORY.md. A4 is an
elementary consequence of A1/A3, not an independent major invention.

## What differs from the closest attention prior

NeurPRISE learns rankings of supplied robust recourse scenarios. Here a public
censored asset is a token, and a focus induces a family of shared-failure subsets
for a quotient of two aggregate rates. Unfocused assets remain in all baseline
costs and uses. The contribution candidate is the quotient-specific omitted
mass calculation, not this representation change or the neural ranking itself.
Generic multiplicative robust scenario-reduction guarantees already exist.

## Claims to exclude from a submission

- First Transformer for censored maintenance, new attention architecture,
  new general robust scenario-reduction principle or new greedy optimizer.
- Attention-certified future risk, causally interpretable attention weights,
  population safety, sensor/intervention counterfactual validity.
- Universal speedup, scalable large-fleet transfer, superiority over the exact
  optimizer, improved measured maintenance economics, 9/10 novelty or a
  quantified RESS acceptance probability.

## Decisive unresolved work

Historical equivalence requires specialist scrutiny of the exact formula against
reliability and robust fractional/scenario-reduction literature, including the
earlier outstanding moment-robust full derivation. A public release can record
attributable disclosure but cannot prove originality. Engineering significance
requires measured failure consequences and a realistic large-cohort comparison
of the complete pipeline. These are limits of available evidence, not tasks
that can be truthfully marked complete by generating another favorable p-value.
