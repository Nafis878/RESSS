## Limits of the final method

1. Conditional deterministic optimization of a recorded cohort, not population
   survival estimation or prospective maintenance safety.
2. Fixed positive integer ages, exact recorded horizons/flags, independent
   Cartesian compatible lifetime completions, common preventive cost one,
   asset-specific positive rational failure premiums. No joint physical
   constraints, downtime, resource scheduling, measurement noise, adaptive
   sensor policies or intervention transitions are modeled.
3. The attention training generator fixes eight unresolved eligible assets and
   two known failures between the age pair. Neither realistic fleet-size
   generalization nor measured maintenance cost improvement was demonstrated.
4. Original model results average seed metrics per cohort; the follow-up uses
   an ensemble of mean logits. These are different protocols and retain
   separate tables. Twelve fitted checkpoints do not supply twelve independent
   population samples.
5. Paired sign-flip tests require exchangeability/symmetry under their null.
   Bootstrap intervals condition on fitted models; they are nominal, not
   simultaneous or training-uncertainty intervals. Fresh confirmation evaluates
   two specified synthetic laws, not new physical environments.
6. Focus enumeration costs 2^k. Greedy's four-asset proposal evaluates 172
   subsets plus 16 final subsets here; attention/premium require 16 final
   subsets plus inference/sorting. These counts are not complete-pipeline time.
7. The hybrid executes the full FPTAS as fallback. This can tighten intervals
   and retains its tolerance guarantee, but adds overhead. No attention or
   hybrid runtime speedup has been established.
8. Tightness of a pair bound does not prove selected-policy-bound sharpness,
   information-theoretic optimality or broad historical novelty. Statistical
   significance cannot establish scientific priority.

## Exact retained contribution for review

For the stated same-world finite-cohort replacement quotient, derive an
omitted-premium certificate valid for any adversarial subset focus, a sharp
uniform factor, premium-optimal focus and minimum certified focus budget.
Intersect focused-search and complete-family FPTAS intervals, retaining attaining
witnesses and the previous (1+epsilon) decision guarantee. The selector is
optional; validation currently favors deterministic greedy. This is a precise
candidate mathematical contribution. Global originality remains unestablished.
