# Mechanism and priority audit

Audit date: 8 October 2026. This extends the frozen audits in `rampe/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md` and `observability/LITERATURE.md`; it does not replace them. Search terms included missing-data worst-case bounds, bounded outcomes, censored maintenance renewal costs, no-overlap policy evaluation, linear-fractional optimization, and limited-information bounds. This is a targeted audit, not a proof that every relevant paper has been found.

The LP equivalence is **project-generated evidence**, not a result copied from these papers. It demonstrates that every implemented interval and certification is reproduced by generic box-constrained linear-fractional optimization. Being representable by an LP alone would not refute an otherwise original formulation. Here the formulation contains no additional structural coupling, new estimator, decision mechanism or statistical guarantee, and its endpoints follow from monotonicity over a rectangular missing-outcome set.

The exact paired resource may still be useful and may warrant a separate benchmark paper. Establishing its priority requires comparison with released evaluation resources, documentation of what new question it supports, and independent review. No new-method priority claim passes this audit.
