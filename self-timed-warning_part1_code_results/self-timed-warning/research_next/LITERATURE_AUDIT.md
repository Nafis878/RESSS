# Mechanism-level literature audit

Search date: 8 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source search, not a systematic review or proof that no future novel mechanism exists. Bibliographic versions matter. Statements below distinguish full-method inspection from abstract-only evidence. No claim of priority is supported.

| Primary source | Reading depth and established mechanism | Implication for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Angelopoulos et al., Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.02814v4) | Full HTML, especially Sections 2 and 4. Controls expected monotone losses; includes shift, multiple-risk and U-risk extensions. | A trajectory critical score, quantile calibration, or a U-statistic objective does not itself establish a new calibration framework. |
| [Angelopoulos et al., Learn then Test](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01052v5) | Full framework and Theorem 1. Valid hypothesis tests plus family-wise error control produce a feasible set; utility can select within it. Empty-set abstention and multiple risks are covered. | Risk gates, cost selection among certified candidates, independent calibration, and abstention are established building blocks. |
| [Laufer-Goldshtein et al., Pareto Testing](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.07913) | Original 2022 manuscript, Sections 5.1–5.3, Algorithm 1 and Proposition 5.1. Independent optimization/testing subsets; Pareto frontier; simultaneous risk constraints and free utility objectives. | Merely adding cost, stability and risk as separate objectives followed by independent testing is insufficient novelty. Not asserted to be the identical maintenance algorithm. |
| [Maurer and Pontil, Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample Variance Penalization, 2009](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.3740) | Primary abstract and [COLT paper](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~colt2009/papers/012.pdf). Data-dependent, variance-sensitive confidence bounds motivate penalizing empirical loss variance. | A validation variance penalty or sample-size-dependent conservatism needs a distinct contribution beyond this general idea. |
| [Cory-Wright and Gómez, Gain Confidence, Reduce Disappointment, 2023 v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.14851v1) | Sections 2.2 and 3; Equation 6 combines leave-one-out error with a hypothesis-stability-based confidence adjustment. Sparse linear regression setting. | Stability-adjusted model selection already exists. A genuinely new operational stability quantity and analysis would be required here. The [current record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14851) has a different title; this comparison explicitly uses v1. |
| [Laroche et al., Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping, ICML 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a/laroche19a.pdf) | Sections 2–3. Baseline behavior constrains improvement where offline evidence is insufficient, with assumptions and performance guarantees in an MDP setting. | Fallback to a baseline under uncertainty is established. This is conceptual overlap, not equivalence of a trajectory tuning problem and offline RL. |
| [HyperTime, original 2023 manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.18421) | Section 3.2: chronological validation, average and worst-fold losses, lexicographic objective and compromise tolerance. | Environment-wise worst-case tuning and cost/stability lexicographic selection are not new in themselves. Battery batch splits are environmental, not a verified chronological stream. |
| [Liang, Zhu and Barber, Conformal prediction after data-dependent model selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07066) | Primary abstract, revised April 2026. Addresses selection bias and finite-sample-valid model selection without extra splitting. | Fixing selection contamination can be scientifically necessary without being a new generic method. Exact applicability to operational failure loss requires separate analysis. |
| [Bao et al., Optimal Model Selection for Conformalized Robust Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04716) | Primary abstract, December 2025 v2. Combines downstream decision-risk model selection and robustness control, with distinct finite/asymptotic variants. | “Decision-aware conformal model selection” is already occupied. No assertion that its optimization matches our renewal-rate objective; full algorithm comparison is required for an extension claim. |
| [Li and Zhu, Sequential Confidence Sets for Coverage-Constrained Conformal Model Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28522) | Primary abstract only, September 2026 preprint. Sequential feasible/optimal pipeline sets, martingale confidence sequences, delayed feedback and outcome-dependent efficiency. | A future delayed-feedback selector must distinguish its estimand, feedback/censoring model and algorithm from this work. Its abstract is not treated as a verified theorem applicable to CAT. |
| [Kumar, Nayak and Pati, maintenance chapter, 2026](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-29501-9_23) | Publisher abstract, as recorded in the frozen prior audit. Conformal maintenance calibration with a safe region and economic threshold optimization. | Do not claim first maintenance cost optimization within a conformal region. Full text remains unavailable in this investigation. |
| [Almeida et al., maintenance paper, 2026](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14759217261445819) | Publisher abstract, as recorded in the frozen prior audit. Miss-rate constraints, maintenance cost and probabilistic/split-conformal calibration. | Operational trajectory timing may differ, but abstract-level differences cannot establish priority. |

The two maintenance papers are retained from the earlier verified audit, rather than presented as newly obtained full-text evidence. They strengthen caution; the rejection of a generic new optimizer already follows from the accessible methodological sources.

## Search scope and decision

Queries covered conformal risk control and decision making; cost-sensitive, risk-aware and stability-aware model selection; robust hyperparameter optimization; adaptive conservatism; selective/abstaining selection; distribution shift and transfer; algorithmic stability; risk instability; and delayed-feedback constrained selection. Searches then narrowed to the actual mechanisms: variance penalties, stability confidence adjustments, independently tested Pareto frontiers, baseline fallback and renewal-cost contrasts.

| Suggested direction in the request | Closest mechanism found | Novelty decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adaptive robustness | Existing 1-SE bootstrap margin; variance-sensitive bounds | A different multiplier or extra tuning parameter is insufficient. |
| Risk-instability-aware tuning | Sample variance penalization; stability-adjusted CV | Generic penalty rejected; an operationally distinct instability functional is unestablished. |
| Transfer-aware calibration | CRC shift extensions; environment-aware validation | Requires justified shift assumptions or target outcome information. A feature drift test does not certify label/conditional stability. |
| Multi-objective cost/risk/stability | Pareto Testing; HyperTime | Objectives alone are insufficient novelty. |
| Selective tuning with fallback | LTT abstention; safe policy improvement | Maintenance adaptation is possible, but the generic mechanism is established. |
| Stability-calibrated conformal selection | CRC plus stability confidence bounds; post-selection conformal work | A union of known components is not yet a distinct method with a demonstrated advantage. |
| Renewal-contrast testing | Algebraic reduction of a ratio comparison plus standard concentration/LTT | A defensible extension/reference calculation; no new theorem or algorithmic novelty established. |

**Verdict: no candidate currently meets the requested combination of identifiable algorithmic novelty, demonstrated failure-mechanism relevance and rigorous validation.** This does not imply that useful maintenance-specific extensions are impossible. It rules out claiming a new ML method from the current evidence and these generic combinations. See the final report for ranked future directions and precise completion status.
